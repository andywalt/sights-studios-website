# Sights Studios — context for AI assistants and crawlers

## What we do
Sights Studios helps organizations with process, product, and people: mapping workflows, improving products and revenue, and team coaching. We also run **AI App Rescue**: fixing, rescuing, and rebuilding web apps that were built with AI coding tools (e.g. Cursor, Bolt, Lovable, v0) when they are broken, stalled, or not production-ready.
